Features

Everything stays in your session.

Lead and harmonies

Click the name next to LISN to switch to Harmonies. It splits the lead vocal from the backing harmonies, Quick or Best quality.

Lead Harmonies

Tempo and key, apart

Drag the Tempo box from 0.5× to 2× and read it in BPM. Shift the key up to 12 semitones either way. One never changes the other.

96 BPM1.30× of 74-3 stKey

A volume for every stem

Each stem has its own bar under its name. Pull the vocals down, push the bass up, double-click for 100%.

All stems in sync

Every stem plays together. Mute or solo any of them to hear exactly what you need.

  2. 2

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it free?+

Yes.

Does it upload my music?+

No. Everything runs on your computer.

Do I need a DAW?+

No. LISN, the desktop app, splits and plays songs on its own. The VST3 is for when you want the stems inside FL Studio or Ableton Live.

Does changing the tempo change the key?+

No. Tempo (0.5× to 2×, shown in BPM) and key (up to 12 semitones up or down) are separate. Double-click either box to reset it.

Is there a Mac version?+

Yes, for Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) on macOS 14 or newer. One installer, LISN-Mac.pkg, puts the VST3 and the desktop app on your Mac. Get it for Mac.

Where are my stems saved?+

In %APPDATA%\StemSplitter\cache. You can drag them anywhere you like.

In ~/Library/Application Support/StemSplitter/cache. You can drag them anywhere you like.

How do I uninstall?+

Go to Windows Settings > Apps, then LISN StemSplitter for the plugin or LISN for the desktop app. Each removes its own AI engine.

Delete these: /Applications/LISN.app, /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/LISN StemSplitter.vst3, the AI engine in /Library/Application Support/LISN, and the Harmonies models in ~/Library/Application Support/LISN. Your stems are in ~/Library/Application Support/StemSplitter.

Why does my Mac block it?+

The beta isn't signed with Apple yet. Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway and type your Mac password.

Why does Windows warn me?+

The beta isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
