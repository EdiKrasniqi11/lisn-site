How it works

Drop. Split. Drag.

  1. 01

    Drop a song

    Load any track straight into the plugin.

    wav mp3 flac aiff ogg
  2. 02

    Split it

    Pick 4 stems, or 6 with guitar and piano.

    Vocals Drums Bass Other Guitar Piano

    Drag the stems

    Pull each stem onto its own track in your DAW.

Install

Up and running in five steps.

  1. 1

    Close your DAW and run the installer.

  2. 2

    If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

    Click More info then Run anyway

    The beta isn't code-signed yet.

  3. 3

    Allow the admin prompt.

    The last step downloads the AI engine. It's about 450 MB, needs internet and takes a few minutes.

  4. 4

    Rescan plugins.

    FL Studio
    Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins
    Ableton Live
    Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan

    with “Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders” turned on.

  5. 5

    Add LISN StemSplitter to a track.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it free?+

Yes.

Does it upload my music?+

No. Everything runs on your PC.

Is there a Mac version?+

Not yet.

Where are my stems saved?+

In %APPDATA%\StemSplitter\cache. You can drag them anywhere you like.

How do I uninstall?+

Go to Windows Settings > Apps > LISN StemSplitter. This also removes the AI engine.

Why does Windows warn me?+

The beta isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
