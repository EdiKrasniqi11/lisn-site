LISN for Mac (test build)

Thank you for trying LISN on a Mac. This is an early build, so I'd love to hear whether it works for you.

Download for Mac

Free · Test build · macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer, Apple Silicon (M1 and newer)

Install on a Mac

  1. 1

    Open LISN-Mac.pkg from Downloads. macOS says it can't be opened: click Done.

    ⚠︎
    “LISN-Mac.pkg” Not OpenedApple could not verify “LISN-Mac.pkg” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
    Done
  2. 2

    Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then type your Mac password.

    Security
    “LISN-Mac.pkg” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
  3. 3

    Click through the installer. The last step downloads the AI engine (about 390 MB, a few minutes).

    Keep the Mac online and let it finish; nothing is stuck, it is just downloading.

What to check

  • The installer finishes.
  • LISN opens from Applications.
  • FL Studio finds LISN StemSplitter (Options › Manage plugins › Find plugins) and it loads.
  • A split works.
  • Harmonies works: click the name next to LISN, drop a song, try Quick.
  • The LISN window: the red, yellow and green buttons sit level with the LISN logo; dragging the top bar moves the window; the Dusk/Midnight buttons respond.
  • If something fails, please include your macOS version and Mac model, a screenshot, and (if the installer failed) the file /Library/Application Support/LISN/engine/setup.log.
  • If FL Studio doesn’t list the plugin, tell us whether FL is set to “Open using Rosetta” (Finder › Get Info).
